# Chilimba App — Deployment Guide
## Get your app live in 15 minutes

---

## What you need
- A computer (phone won't work for this part)
- A Gmail or GitHub account
- The files in this folder

---

## Step 1 — Create a GitHub account (2 min)
1. Go to **github.com**
2. Click "Sign up"
3. Use your Gmail to register
4. Verify your email

---

## Step 2 — Upload your project to GitHub (5 min)
1. Once logged in, click the **+** icon (top right) → "New repository"
2. Name it: `chilimba-app`
3. Set it to **Public**
4. Click "Create repository"
5. On the next page, click **"uploading an existing file"**
6. Drag and drop ALL the files from this folder into the upload area
   - Make sure you upload the folders too (src, public)
7. Click "Commit changes"

---

## Step 3 — Deploy to Vercel (5 min)
1. Go to **vercel.com**
2. Click "Sign Up" → choose "Continue with GitHub"
3. Click **"Add New Project"**
4. Find `chilimba-app` in your list → click "Import"
5. Leave all settings as default
6. Click **"Deploy"**
7. Wait about 2 minutes ⏳

---

## Step 4 — Get your link
After deployment, Vercel gives you a link like:
**https://chilimba-app.vercel.app**

That's your live app! Share it on WhatsApp with your pilot group.

---

## Step 5 — Install on phones
Tell your group members:
1. Open the link in **Chrome** (Android) or **Safari** (iPhone)
2. Tap the share/menu button
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. It installs like a real app — no Play Store needed!

---

## Important notes
- The app is **free to host** on Vercel
- Data currently saves on each person's phone separately
- The treasurer should be the main person recording transactions
- Use the WhatsApp share button inside the app to send reports to the group
